# BoI Provision 26 March 2019 — Sezione VI: Matrice di determinazione del rischio residuo

Textual transcription of the residual-risk determination matrix from Banca d'Italia
Provision of 26 March 2019 on AML organisation, procedures and internal controls
(consolidated to 23 July 2025), Section VI. In the source the matrix is a colour-coded
figure; only the four anchor combinations carry an explicit text label, the remaining
cells are colour-banded. This file transcribes the methodology so it is captured by
text-only extraction.

## Obligation

The intermediary MUST determine the residual money-laundering / terrorist-financing risk
by combining two axes, each scored 1–4, and assign one of four residual-risk levels. The
resulting residual-risk level MUST drive the intensity of customer due diligence, ongoing
monitoring, and internal controls.

## Axis A — Rischio inerente (inherent risk)

1 = basso (low); 2 = medio basso (medium-low); 3 = medio alto (medium-high); 4 = alto (high).

## Axis B — Vulnerabilità nel sistema organizzativo e dei controlli (vulnerability of the organisational and control system)

1 = non significativa (not significant); 2 = poco significativa (slightly significant);
3 = abbastanza significativa (fairly significant); 4 = molto significativa (very significant).

## Residual-risk output levels

(1) rischio residuo non significativo; (2) rischio residuo basso; (3) rischio residuo medio;
(4) rischio residuo elevato.

## Anchor combinations explicitly labelled in the source

- inherent 1 (basso) × vulnerability 1 (non significativa) → residual non significativo (1)
- low inherent / low vulnerability band → residual basso (2)
- mid band → residual medio (3)
- inherent 4 (alto) × vulnerability 4 (molto significativa) → residual elevato (4)

Both higher inherent risk and higher control-system vulnerability increase the residual-risk
level; the remaining cells follow the colour-banded gradient between these anchors.
