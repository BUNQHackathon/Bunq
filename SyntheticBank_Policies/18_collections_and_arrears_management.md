# Collections and Arrears Management Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Collections Manager
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind manages customer accounts that fall into arrears, including forbearance, external collection, and recovery action.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all retail and SME credit accounts held by Northwind that fall into arrears.

## 3. Policy Statements

18.1 The Collections Manager must contact a customer within five business days of an account first falling into arrears to discuss repayment options.

18.2 Collections staff must offer a customer in financial difficulty a forbearance option, such as a repayment plan or temporary payment reduction, before initiating formal recovery action.

18.3 The Collections Manager must not apply collections charges that exceed the amount permitted under the customer's contractual terms and applicable consumer credit law.

18.4 Accounts identified as involving a vulnerable customer must be routed to specially trained Collections staff and must not be referred to external debt collection agencies without the Collections Manager's approval.

18.5 The Collections Manager must review the performance and treatment-of-customer standards of any external debt collection agency engaged by Northwind at least once every twelve months.

18.6 Collections staff must record every customer contact and repayment arrangement in the Northwind Collections System.

18.7 An account must not be referred for legal recovery action until the Collections Manager has confirmed that all applicable pre-action requirements and forbearance options have been exhausted.

18.8 The Collections Manager must suspend the accrual of further collections charges once an account has been referred for legal recovery action.

18.9 Collections staff must not contact a customer about arrears more than three times in a seven-day period without the customer's consent.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Collections Manager:** owns this policy and approves referrals to external debt collection agencies.
- **Collections staff:** contact customers in arrears and record repayment arrangements.
