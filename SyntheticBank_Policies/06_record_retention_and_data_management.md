# Record Retention and Data Management Policy

**Version:** 2.2
**Owner:** Data Protection Officer
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how long Northwind retains customer due diligence records, transaction records, and suspicious activity referral records, and how those records are stored and protected.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all customer due diligence documentation, transaction records, and internal SAR referrals held by Northwind, in any format.

## 3. Policy Statements

6.1 Customer due diligence records, transaction records, and internal SAR referrals must be retained for a period of five years from the end of the business relationship or the date of the occasional transaction.

6.2 All retained records must be stored in the Northwind Document Vault in a format that preserves their integrity and allows retrieval within five business days of a request from a competent authority.

6.3 Access to records held in the Northwind Document Vault must be restricted to Compliance, Internal Audit, and personnel expressly authorised by the Data Protection Officer.

6.4 Records must be deleted no later than six months after expiry of the applicable retention period, unless subject to a litigation hold or regulatory request.

6.5 Customer Onboarding must ensure that customer due diligence data held in the Customer Risk Engine is reviewed and, where necessary, refreshed at intervals determined by the customer's risk rating.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Data Protection Officer:** owns this policy and authorises access to the Northwind Document Vault.
- **Compliance and Internal Audit:** access records for supervisory and investigatory purposes.
