# Incident Management Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Head of Incident Management
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind identifies, classifies, escalates, and resolves operational and security incidents.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all operational and security incidents affecting Northwind's systems, services, or customers.

## 3. Policy Statements

25.1 Any employee who identifies a suspected operational or security incident must report it to the Head of Incident Management through the Northwind Incident Log without delay.

25.2 The Head of Incident Management must classify every reported incident by severity within two business hours of it being logged.

25.3 An incident classified as critical must trigger activation of the incident response team and notification to the Management Board within one business hour of classification.

25.4 The Head of Incident Management must coordinate root-cause analysis for every critical incident and document remedial actions in the Northwind Incident Log.

25.5 Where an incident affects customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, or sanctions screening systems, the Head of Incident Management must notify the Chief Compliance Officer without delay.

25.6 The Head of Incident Management must review closed incidents on a quarterly basis to identify recurring root causes and systemic control weaknesses.

25.7 The Head of Incident Management must maintain a communication log for every critical incident recording all internal and external notifications made during the incident.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Incident Management:** owns this policy and coordinates the incident response team.
- **All employees:** report suspected incidents without delay.
