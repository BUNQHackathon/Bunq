# Conduct Risk and Fair Treatment of Customers Policy

**Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Head of Conduct Risk
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the controls Northwind operates to ensure customers are treated fairly across the product lifecycle, from design and marketing through to sale and post-sale servicing.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all retail and SME products, marketing communications, and customer-facing sales activity carried out by Northwind.

## 3. Policy Statements

16.1 The Head of Conduct Risk must ensure that new products and significant product changes are approved through a product governance process that assesses the likely target market and the risk of customer harm before launch.

16.2 Marketing and promotional communications must be reviewed and approved by the Head of Conduct Risk before publication to ensure they are clear, fair, and not misleading.

16.3 Customer-facing staff must not receive sales incentives that could reasonably be expected to encourage the sale of unsuitable products.

16.4 The Head of Conduct Risk must monitor product outcomes, including cancellation rates, complaint rates, and customer detriment indicators, at least quarterly.

16.5 Vulnerable customers identified through customer contact or account activity must be flagged in the Customer Risk Engine so that customer-facing staff can apply additional support measures.

16.6 The Head of Conduct Risk must report material conduct risk findings and remediation plans to the Management Board at least quarterly.

16.7 Northwind must review the fairness of contractual terms and fee structures applicable to retail customers at least once every twelve months.

16.8 The Head of Conduct Risk must ensure that customer-facing staff scripts and decision trees do not steer customers toward products that are unsuitable for their circumstances.

16.9 Northwind must publish its complaints and conduct outcome metrics internally to the Management Board to support the annual product governance review.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Conduct Risk:** owns this policy, approves marketing, and monitors product outcomes.
- **Customer-facing staff:** apply fair treatment principles in sales and servicing interactions.
