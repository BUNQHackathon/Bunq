# Suspicious Activity Reporting Policy

**Version:** 3.3
**Owner:** Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind employees identify, escalate, and report suspicious activity that may relate to money laundering or terrorist financing.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, agents, and outsourcing providers who become aware of activity giving rise to suspicion in the course of their duties for Northwind.

## 3. Policy Statements

5.1 Every employee who knows, suspects, or has reasonable grounds to suspect that a transaction is related to money laundering or terrorist financing must report it to the MLRO without delay through the internal SAR Referral Form.

5.2 The MLRO must review every internal SAR referral and decide, within two business days, whether to file an external suspicious activity report.

5.3 Where the MLRO determines that a suspicious activity report is warranted, the MLRO must file the report with the Financial Intelligence Unit-Netherlands (FIU-Nederland) through its goAML portal.

5.4 Employees must not disclose to the customer, or to any third party, that a suspicious activity report has been made or is being considered.

5.5 Where a transaction is flagged as potentially suspicious, the MLRO may place the transaction under review for up to five business days before releasing or rejecting it, unless a legal or regulatory obligation requires it to proceed.

5.6 The MLRO must maintain a register of all internal SAR referrals, including those not escalated externally, together with the reasons for each decision.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **MLRO:** owns this policy, reviews internal referrals, and files external reports.
- **All employees:** report suspected suspicious activity without delay and observe confidentiality.
