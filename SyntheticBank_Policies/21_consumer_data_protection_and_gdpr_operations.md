# Consumer Data Protection and GDPR Operations Policy

**Version:** 1.4
**Owner:** Data Protection Officer
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind processes, protects, and manages customer personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all customer personal data processed by Northwind, in any format, including data processed by outsourcing providers on Northwind's behalf.

## 3. Policy Statements

21.1 The Data Protection Officer must maintain a record of processing activities for all customer personal data processed by Northwind, in accordance with Northwind's data protection obligations.

21.2 Northwind must obtain a documented lawful basis before processing customer personal data for any purpose beyond performance of the customer contract.

21.3 A customer exercising a right of access, rectification, erasure, or data portability must receive a substantive response from the Data Protection Officer within thirty calendar days of the request.

21.4 The Data Protection Officer must conduct a data protection impact assessment before Northwind deploys any new system or process involving large-scale processing of customer personal data.

21.5 Personal data must not be transferred outside the European Economic Area unless an approved transfer mechanism is in place and the Data Protection Officer has approved the transfer.

21.6 Northwind must notify the Data Protection Officer of any suspected personal data breach within twenty-four hours of discovery, and the Data Protection Officer must assess whether the breach requires notification to the competent supervisory authority within the statutory deadline.

21.7 The Data Protection Officer must maintain data retention schedules for each category of customer personal data and ensure data is deleted or anonymised once the applicable retention period expires.

21.8 Marketing communications to customers must not be sent unless the customer has provided valid consent or Northwind otherwise has a lawful basis, and the customer must be able to withdraw consent at any time.

21.9 The Data Protection Officer must approve any use of customer personal data for analytics or model development that goes beyond the original purpose for which the data was collected.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Data Protection Officer:** owns this policy and approves cross-border transfers and impact assessments.
- **All employees:** report suspected personal data breaches to the Data Protection Officer without delay.
