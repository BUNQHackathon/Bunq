# Training and Awareness Policy

**Version:** 1.6
**Owner:** Head of Learning & Development
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out Northwind's requirements for anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing training for employees, management, and governance bodies.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Northwind employees, and to members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board.

## 3. Policy Statements

9.1 All employees must complete Northwind's anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing training on the Northwind Learning Hub within thirty days of joining and at least once every twelve months thereafter.

9.2 Employees in customer-facing, transaction monitoring, and sanctions screening roles must complete additional role-specific training modules covering red flags relevant to their function.

9.3 The anti-money laundering training curriculum must cover the EU Anti-Money Laundering Directives, Dutch implementing regulation, Northwind's internal policies, and the typologies published by the Financial Action Task Force.

9.4 The Head of Learning & Development must track training completion rates and report any employee who has not completed mandatory training within the deadline to that employee's manager and to Compliance.

9.5 Members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board must complete anti-money laundering training tailored to their governance responsibilities at least once every twelve months.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Learning & Development:** owns the training curriculum and tracks completion.
- **All employees:** complete mandatory and role-specific training.
