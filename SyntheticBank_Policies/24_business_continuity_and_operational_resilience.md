# Business Continuity and Operational Resilience Policy

**Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Business Continuity Manager
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind maintains its ability to deliver critical business functions during and after a disruption.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all critical business functions performed by Northwind, including those delivered by outsourcing providers on its behalf.

## 3. Policy Statements

24.1 The Business Continuity Manager must maintain a business continuity plan covering each critical business function, including customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, and payment processing.

24.2 Northwind must test its business continuity and disaster recovery plans at least once every twelve months, using a scenario that includes loss of a primary data centre.

24.3 The Business Continuity Manager must define, for each critical business function, a maximum tolerable period of disruption and a corresponding recovery time objective.

24.4 Critical third-party service providers must be included in Northwind's business continuity testing, or the Business Continuity Manager must obtain independent assurance of the provider's own continuity arrangements.

24.5 The Business Continuity Manager must report the outcome of business continuity tests, including any identified gaps and remediation actions, to the Management Board.

24.6 Northwind must maintain an up-to-date crisis communication plan identifying who is authorised to communicate with customers, staff, and regulators during a significant disruption.

24.7 The Business Continuity Manager must maintain an alternative processing site or cloud failover capability for each system supporting a critical business function.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Business Continuity Manager:** owns this policy and the business continuity testing programme.
- **Management Board:** reviews the outcome of business continuity tests.
