# HR Onboarding and Background Checks Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Head of People
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all employees, contractors, and agency staff

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the pre-employment screening and onboarding controls Northwind applies before a new employee, contractor, or agency worker starts work.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all prospective employees, contractors, and agency staff engaged to perform work for Northwind.

## 3. Policy Statements

26.1 The Head of People must complete identity verification and a criminal record check for every new employee before that employee's start date.

26.2 A new employee whose role provides access to customer funds, payment systems, or customer personal data must complete a credit reference check as part of pre-employment screening.

26.3 The Head of People must verify a candidate's employment history and professional qualifications for any role at manager level or above before an offer of employment is confirmed.

26.4 An employee moving into a role with materially different access rights or regulatory responsibilities must be subject to a refreshed background check before taking up the new role.

26.5 The Head of People must maintain records of pre-employment screening outcomes and retain them for the duration of the employee's employment plus one year.

26.6 Contractors and agency staff performing functions equivalent to a Northwind employee must be subject to the same pre-employment screening standards before commencing work.

26.7 The Head of People must ensure that access to Northwind's HR and payroll systems is restricted to authorised Head of People staff and IT administrators.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of People:** owns this policy and completes pre-employment screening.
- **Hiring managers:** confirm role-specific screening requirements before an offer is made.
