# Credit Risk and Affordability Assessment Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Head of Credit Risk
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind assesses a customer's creditworthiness and affordability before extending credit, and how credit risk appetite is set and monitored.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all retail and SME credit products offered by Northwind, including overdrafts, credit cards, and instalment loans.

## 3. Policy Statements

17.1 Before extending credit to a retail or SME customer, a Credit Underwriter must assess the customer's income, expenditure, and existing debt obligations to determine affordability.

17.2 The Northwind Credit Decision Engine must decline any credit application where the assessed debt-to-income ratio exceeds the threshold set by the Head of Credit Risk.

17.3 Credit decisions must be based on information obtained from at least one recognised credit reference agency, refreshed no earlier than thirty days before the decision.

17.4 The Head of Credit Risk must review and approve credit risk appetite and underwriting criteria at least once every twelve months.

17.5 Where a customer's circumstances materially change after credit is extended, the Credit Underwriter must reassess affordability before agreeing to any increase in credit limit.

17.6 The Head of Credit Risk must monitor the credit portfolio's arrears and default rates on a monthly basis and report material deterioration to the Management Board.

17.7 Automated credit decisions made by the Northwind Credit Decision Engine must be capable of human review, and a customer who is declined credit must be informed of the principal reason for the decision.

17.8 The Head of Credit Risk must ensure that affordability assessments take into account a reasonable buffer for changes in interest rates on variable-rate credit products.

17.9 Credit files, including the affordability assessment and supporting evidence, must be retained in the Northwind Document Vault for the duration of the credit agreement plus six years.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Credit Risk:** owns this policy and sets credit risk appetite.
- **Credit Underwriters:** perform affordability assessments and credit decisions.
