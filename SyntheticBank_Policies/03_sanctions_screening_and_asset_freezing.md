# Sanctions Screening and Asset Freezing Policy

**Version:** 2.4
**Owner:** Head of Sanctions & Screening
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the controls Northwind operates to screen customers and payments against applicable sanctions lists and to freeze funds or economic resources where a confirmed match is identified.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all customers, beneficial owners, counterparties, and payments processed by Northwind, including payments executed under freedom-of-services passporting.

## 3. Policy Statements

3.1 The Watchlist Screening Service must screen every payment, at the moment of initiation, against the consolidated EU and UN sanctions and asset-freezing lists before the payment is released.

3.2 Customer Onboarding must screen every prospective customer and beneficial owner against the consolidated EU and UN sanctions lists before the business relationship is established, and must reject onboarding where a confirmed match is found.

3.3 The Watchlist Screening Service must re-screen the entire active customer base against updated EU and UN sanctions lists within 24 hours of each list update.

3.4 Where the Watchlist Screening Service returns a confirmed positive match, the Sanctions Officer must immediately freeze the funds or economic resources involved and escalate the case to the Head of Sanctions & Screening within one business day.

3.5 The Head of Sanctions & Screening must notify Northwind's home-state supervisory authorities, being De Nederlandsche Bank and the Dutch Ministry of Finance, of any confirmed asset freeze within the timeframe prescribed by Dutch sanctions law.

3.6 The Sanctions Officer must update the Watchlist Screening Service's reference lists within one business day of publication of a new or amended EU or UN sanctions listing.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Sanctions & Screening:** owns this policy and notifies home-state authorities of confirmed freezes.
- **Sanctions Officer:** actions confirmed matches, freezes funds, and maintains reference lists.
- **Customer Onboarding:** performs pre-onboarding sanctions screening.
