# Tax Reporting (CRS and FATCA) Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Head of Tax Operations
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the controls Northwind operates to identify reportable accounts and to report account information to the competent tax authorities under the Common Reporting Standard ("CRS") and the US Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act ("FATCA").

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all retail and SME accounts opened by Northwind, including accounts opened by customers served under freedom-of-services passporting.

## 3. Policy Statements

20.1 Customer Onboarding must collect a valid self-certification of tax residency from every new customer before the business relationship is established.

20.2 The Northwind Tax Reporting System must classify each customer as reportable or non-reportable under the Common Reporting Standard and the US Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act based on the self-certification and other account information held.

20.3 Where a self-certification appears inconsistent with other information held on the customer, the Head of Tax Operations must obtain a valid explanation or amended self-certification before relying on it.

20.4 The Head of Tax Operations must transmit reportable account information to the competent tax authority annually, within the statutory deadline applicable to that authority.

20.5 Existing customer accounts must be reviewed for a change in circumstances that affects tax residency status at least once every twelve months.

20.6 The Head of Tax Operations must retain self-certifications and supporting documentation for a period of six years from the end of the calendar year in which the account was closed.

20.7 Northwind must not open an account for a prospective customer who refuses to provide a valid self-certification of tax residency.

20.8 The Head of Tax Operations must validate the completeness of reportable account data against the Northwind Tax Reporting System before annual transmission to the competent tax authority.

20.9 Where a customer subsequently provides a corrected self-certification, the Head of Tax Operations must update the customer's reportable status within thirty calendar days.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Tax Operations:** owns this policy and transmits reportable account data to competent tax authorities.
- **Customer Onboarding:** collects self-certifications of tax residency at account opening.
