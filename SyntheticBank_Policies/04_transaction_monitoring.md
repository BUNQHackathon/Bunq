# Transaction Monitoring Policy

**Version:** 2.1
**Owner:** Head of Transaction Monitoring
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Northwind monitors customer transactions on an ongoing basis to detect activity that is inconsistent with a customer's expected profile or that may indicate money laundering or terrorist financing.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all payment accounts, e-money accounts, and card products offered by Northwind.

## 3. Policy Statements

4.1 The Customer Risk Engine must continuously monitor all customer transactions throughout the business relationship to ensure they are consistent with Northwind's knowledge of the customer, its business, and its risk profile.

4.2 Transaction Monitoring analysts must review and disposition every system-generated alert within five business days of generation.

4.3 The Head of Transaction Monitoring must review and, where necessary, recalibrate monitoring scenario thresholds at least annually based on the outcomes of the enterprise-wide risk assessment.

4.4 Any alert that Transaction Monitoring analysts cannot reasonably resolve as consistent with the customer's expected activity must be escalated to the Financial Crime Investigations Team within two business days.

4.5 The Customer Risk Engine must flag for review any pattern of frequent or unusual cash deposits or withdrawals inconsistent with the customer's declared profile.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Transaction Monitoring:** owns monitoring scenarios and thresholds.
- **Transaction Monitoring analysts:** review, disposition, and escalate alerts.
- **Financial Crime Investigations Team:** investigates escalated alerts.
