# Internal Fraud and Staff Whistleblowing Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Head of Internal Investigations
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all employees, contractors, and agents

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the controls Northwind operates to prevent, detect, and investigate fraud or dishonesty committed by its employees, contractors, or agents, and the whistleblowing channel through which such conduct can be reported.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Northwind employees, contractors, and agents, and to any suspected act of internal fraud, theft, or dishonesty affecting Northwind, its customers, or its assets.

## 3. Policy Statements

14.1 Northwind must maintain a confidential whistleblowing channel, operated by an independent third-party provider, through which employees, contractors, and agents can report suspected internal fraud or dishonesty.

14.2 The Head of Internal Investigations must acknowledge receipt of a whistleblowing report within two business days and open a case in Northwind Case Manager.

14.3 Any employee found to have engaged in internal fraud must be referred to Human Resources for disciplinary action and reported to the Head of Internal Investigations for possible referral to law enforcement.

14.4 Employees with access to customer funds, cash, or payment systems must be subject to segregation-of-duties controls preventing any single employee from initiating and approving the same payment.

14.5 The Head of Internal Investigations must report the outcome of substantiated whistleblowing investigations to the Audit Committee on a quarterly basis.

14.6 Employees who raise a report in good faith must not be subject to retaliation, disciplinary action, or detrimental treatment as a result of having raised the report.

14.7 The Head of Internal Investigations must maintain a register of all internal fraud incidents, including estimated financial loss and remedial action taken.

14.8 Access rights of any employee under active investigation for suspected internal fraud must be suspended by the Head of Internal Investigations pending the outcome of the investigation.

14.9 The Head of Internal Investigations must ensure that whistleblowing case files are accessible only to personnel directly involved in the investigation.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Internal Investigations:** owns this policy and manages internal fraud investigations.
- **Human Resources:** actions disciplinary consequences for substantiated internal fraud.
