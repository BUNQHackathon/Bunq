# Information Security and Access Management Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the controls Northwind operates to protect its systems and data, and to manage user access to those systems.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Northwind systems that store or process customer, payment, or employee data, and to every employee, contractor, and agent with access to those systems.

## 3. Policy Statements

22.1 The Chief Information Security Officer must ensure that access to systems processing customer or payment data is granted on a least-privilege, role-based basis and approved by the relevant system owner.

22.2 User access rights must be reviewed at least once every six months, and any access no longer required must be revoked within five business days of the review.

22.3 Privileged access to production systems must require multi-factor authentication and must be logged in a manner that prevents the privileged user from altering or deleting the log.

22.4 The Chief Information Security Officer must ensure that a departing employee's system access is revoked no later than their last working day.

22.5 Northwind must conduct a penetration test of its customer-facing digital channels at least once every twelve months and remediate critical findings within thirty calendar days.

22.6 The Chief Information Security Officer must maintain an inventory of all systems that store or process customer personal or financial data, classified by sensitivity.

22.7 Passwords for Northwind systems must meet minimum complexity requirements and must not be shared between users under any circumstances.

22.8 The Chief Information Security Officer must ensure security event logs from systems supporting customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, and sanctions screening are retained for at least one year and protected from tampering.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Chief Information Security Officer:** owns this policy and the access review and penetration testing programme.
- **System owners:** approve access requests for systems under their ownership.
