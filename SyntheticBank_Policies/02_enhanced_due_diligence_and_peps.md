# Enhanced Due Diligence and Politically Exposed Persons Policy

**Version:** 3.0
**Owner:** Head of Financial Crime Risk
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the enhanced due diligence ("EDD") measures Northwind applies to higher-risk customers, including politically exposed persons ("PEPs"), and to correspondent banking relationships.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all customers, relationships, and transactions that the Customer Risk Engine classifies as high risk, and to all PEPs, their family members, and known close associates.

## 3. Policy Statements

2.1 The Customer Risk Engine must apply enhanced due diligence measures to any customer, relationship, or transaction assessed as high risk of money laundering or terrorist financing.

2.2 Customer Onboarding must screen every new and existing customer, beneficial owner, and authorised signatory against an internationally recognised politically exposed persons database at onboarding and no less than quarterly thereafter.

2.3 A member of Northwind's senior management must approve, in writing, the establishment or continuation of any business relationship with a customer, beneficial owner, or beneficiary identified as a politically exposed person before the relationship is opened or continued.

2.4 For any politically exposed person relationship, the Relationship Manager must establish and document the source of wealth and the source of funds used in the relationship before the first transaction is executed.

2.5 Politically exposed person relationships must be subject to enhanced ongoing monitoring, including a full relationship review at least once every twelve months.

2.6 Northwind must not open or maintain, directly or indirectly, correspondent banking relationships with a shell bank or with a respondent institution known to permit its accounts to be used by a shell bank.

2.7 Before establishing a cross-border correspondent relationship, the Head of Financial Crime Risk must approve the relationship following an assessment of the respondent institution's ownership, management, and AML control environment.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Financial Crime Risk:** owns this policy and approves correspondent banking relationships.
- **Senior management:** approves PEP relationships before onboarding or continuation.
- **Relationship Manager:** documents source of wealth and source of funds for PEP customers.
