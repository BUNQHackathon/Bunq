# Account Closure and Dormancy Policy

**Version:** 1.1
**Owner:** Head of Account Servicing
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind identifies and manages dormant accounts, and how it processes customer-requested and Northwind-initiated account closures.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all retail and SME payment and e-money accounts held by Northwind.

## 3. Policy Statements

19.1 The Head of Account Servicing must classify a customer account as dormant where no customer-initiated transaction has occurred for a period of twenty-four months.

19.2 Northwind must attempt to contact a customer through at least two channels before classifying their account as dormant.

19.3 Payments and withdrawals from a dormant account must be subject to additional identity re-verification before being processed.

19.4 A customer may close their account at any time, and Northwind must complete the closure, including return of any remaining balance, within five business days of the closure request.

19.5 The Head of Account Servicing must not close a customer account solely on the grounds of the customer's nationality, country of residence, or the exercise of a legal right, without a documented risk-based justification.

19.6 Where Northwind decides to close an account on its own initiative, it must provide the customer with at least two months' written notice, except where immediate closure is required to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation.

19.7 Balances remaining on accounts closed for dormancy must be transferred to a suspense ledger and remain reclaimable by the customer or their legal successor.

19.8 The Head of Account Servicing must review the dormant account population at least twice a year to identify accounts eligible for reactivation outreach.

19.9 The Head of Account Servicing must ensure that fees are not charged on a dormant account beyond the fees that applied before the account became dormant.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Account Servicing:** owns this policy and approves Northwind-initiated account closures.
- **Customer Onboarding:** performs identity re-verification for activity on dormant accounts.
