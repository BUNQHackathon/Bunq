# Outsourcing and Third-Party Risk Policy

**Version:** 1.4
**Owner:** Head of Third-Party Risk
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all outsourcing providers and agents performing functions on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the controls Northwind applies when it outsources, or engages agents to perform, functions relevant to anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing compliance.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to any outsourcing provider or agent that performs customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, or payment services on Northwind's behalf.

## 3. Policy Statements

10.1 Before engaging any outsourcing provider or agent who performs customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, or screening activities on Northwind's behalf, the Head of Third-Party Risk must complete a due diligence assessment of the provider's anti-money laundering controls.

10.2 Every outsourcing arrangement involving anti-money laundering functions must be documented in a written agreement that specifies Northwind's right to audit, access records, and terminate the arrangement for compliance failures.

10.3 Northwind must review each outsourcing provider's performance and compliance with anti-money laundering requirements at least once every twelve months.

10.4 An outsourcing provider must not sub-delegate any anti-money laundering function to a further third party without the prior written consent of the Head of Third-Party Risk.

10.5 The Head of Third-Party Risk must maintain a documented exit plan for each material outsourcing arrangement to ensure continuity of anti-money laundering controls if the arrangement is terminated.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Third-Party Risk:** owns this policy and approves outsourcing arrangements.
- **Outsourcing providers and agents:** comply with the terms of their written agreement with Northwind.
