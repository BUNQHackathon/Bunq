# AML Governance and Internal Controls Policy

**Version:** 3.1
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the governance structure, oversight roles, and internal control mechanisms that support Northwind's anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing ("AML/CTF") programme.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to Northwind's Management Board, Supervisory Board, Compliance function, and Internal Audit function.

## 3. Policy Statements

8.1 The Management Board must appoint a Money Laundering Reporting Officer with sufficient seniority, independence, and resources to oversee Northwind's anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing programme.

8.2 Internal Audit must independently test the design and operating effectiveness of Northwind's anti-money laundering controls at least once every twelve months and report findings directly to the Audit Committee.

8.3 Northwind must maintain a confidential and, where requested, anonymous whistleblowing channel through which employees can report actual or suspected breaches of anti-money laundering policy without fear of retaliation.

8.4 The Chief Compliance Officer must review and, where necessary, update this policy framework at least once every twelve months or following any material change in applicable law.

8.5 The Chief Compliance Officer must report to the Management Board, at least quarterly, on the effectiveness of the anti-money laundering control framework, key risk indicators, and material incidents.

8.6 Members of the Supervisory Board must report to the Chief Compliance Officer, without delay, any suspicious activity or material control weakness identified in the course of their duties.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Management Board:** appoints the MLRO and approves the AML/CTF programme.
- **Chief Compliance Officer:** owns this policy and reports to the Management Board.
- **Internal Audit:** independently tests the AML control framework.
- **Supervisory Board:** reports suspicious activity or control weaknesses to the Chief Compliance Officer.
