# Card Dispute and Chargeback Handling Policy

**Version:** 1.1
**Owner:** Card Operations Manager
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind logs, investigates, and resolves customer-initiated card disputes, and how it processes chargebacks under applicable card scheme rules.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all debit and credit card transactions processed by Northwind, including transactions disputed by customers served under freedom-of-services passporting.

## 3. Policy Statements

12.1 The Card Operations Manager must log every customer dispute in Northwind Case Manager within one business day of receipt.

12.2 Card Operations staff must attempt to resolve a customer dispute directly with the merchant's acquiring bank before escalating a chargeback, where scheme rules permit.

12.3 A chargeback must be filed with the relevant card scheme within the time limit prescribed by that scheme's operating regulations, and no later than 120 days from the transaction date.

12.4 The Card Operations Manager must provide the customer with a provisional credit for a disputed transaction within ten business days of the dispute being logged, pending the outcome of the chargeback.

12.5 Card Operations staff must retain all dispute evidence, including transaction records and correspondence with the customer and the acquiring bank, for the duration of the chargeback and any subsequent arbitration.

12.6 Where a chargeback is represented by the merchant's acquiring bank, the Card Operations Manager must review the representment and decide, within five business days, whether to pursue arbitration or accept the loss.

12.7 The Card Operations Manager must report chargeback volumes and win/loss rates to the Head of Fraud Operations on a monthly basis.

12.8 Card Operations staff must flag any merchant with a chargeback rate exceeding the threshold set by the relevant card scheme to the Head of Fraud Operations for review.

12.9 The Card Operations Manager must ensure customers are notified of the outcome of a dispute in writing within five business days of the decision being made.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Card Operations Manager:** owns this policy and decides on arbitration and representment outcomes.
- **Card Operations staff:** log disputes, gather evidence, and liaise with acquiring banks.
