# Customer Complaints Handling Policy

**Version:** 1.1
**Owner:** Complaints Manager
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind logs, investigates, and resolves complaints received from customers.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to any expression of dissatisfaction, written or verbal, made by a customer about a Northwind product, service, or member of staff.

## 3. Policy Statements

15.1 The Complaints Manager must log every customer complaint in the Northwind Complaints Register within one business day of receipt.

15.2 Northwind must issue a written acknowledgement of a complaint to the customer within five business days of receipt.

15.3 Northwind must issue a final response to a complaint within fifteen business days of receipt, or explain to the customer the reason for any delay and the revised expected timeframe.

15.4 Where Northwind cannot resolve a complaint to the customer's satisfaction within the applicable timeframe, the Complaints Manager must inform the customer of their right to refer the complaint to the competent financial ombudsman or alternative dispute resolution body.

15.5 The Complaints Manager must analyse complaint root causes on a quarterly basis and report recurring themes to the Head of Conduct Risk.

15.6 Complaints relating to suspected fraud, unauthorised transactions, or account takeover must be routed to the Fraud Operations Manager in addition to being logged in the Northwind Complaints Register.

15.7 Complaint records must be retained in the Northwind Document Vault for a period of six years from the date of final resolution.

15.8 The Complaints Manager must provide complaint volume and resolution-time metrics to the Management Board at least quarterly.

15.9 The Complaints Manager must ensure complaints handling staff receive training on Northwind's complaints procedure at least once every twelve months.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Complaints Manager:** owns this policy and reports complaint trends to the Management Board.
- **Customer-facing staff:** log and escalate complaints received directly from customers.
