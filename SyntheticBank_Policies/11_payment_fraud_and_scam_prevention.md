# Payment Fraud and Scam Prevention Policy

**Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Head of Fraud Operations
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the controls Northwind operates to detect and prevent payment fraud, account-takeover-driven fraud, and authorised push-payment scams affecting its customers.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all payment accounts, e-money accounts, and card products offered by Northwind, and to every outbound payment instruction submitted through a Northwind channel.

## 3. Policy Statements

11.1 The Fraud Operations Manager must ensure that the Northwind Fraud Engine screens every outbound payment in real time against known fraud typologies before the payment is released.

11.2 Payments flagged by the Northwind Fraud Engine as high risk of an authorised push-payment scam must be held for manual review by a Fraud Analyst before release, and the customer must be contacted through a verified channel to confirm the payment's legitimacy.

11.3 Fraud Analysts must triage and disposition every Northwind Fraud Engine alert within four business hours of generation.

11.4 Where a customer reports an unauthorised transaction, the Fraud Operations Manager must open a case in Northwind Case Manager and provisionally refund the customer within ten business days unless there are reasonable grounds to suspect the customer acted fraudulently or with gross negligence.

11.5 The Fraud Operations Manager must submit a report of confirmed scam and fraud losses to the Head of Financial Crime Risk on a monthly basis to inform the enterprise-wide risk assessment.

11.6 Northwind must participate in an industry fraud-intelligence-sharing scheme and incorporate shared mule-account indicators into the Northwind Fraud Engine's detection scenarios within five business days of receipt.

11.7 Customers must be able to report suspected fraud or scams through a 24/7 channel, and the Fraud Operations Manager must acknowledge each report within one business day.

11.8 Where a payment is suspected to be the proceeds of a romance, investment, or impersonation scam, the Fraud Operations Manager must attempt to recall the funds from the receiving institution within one business day of confirmation.

11.9 The Fraud Operations Manager must review and recalibrate Northwind Fraud Engine detection scenarios at least once every six months based on observed fraud typologies and loss trends.

11.10 Northwind must conduct a post-incident review of every confirmed scam loss exceeding EUR 5,000 and report lessons learned to the Head of Fraud Operations within ten business days.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Fraud Operations:** owns this policy, oversees the Northwind Fraud Engine, and reports fraud losses.
- **Fraud Analysts:** triage alerts, verify suspicious payments, and manage confirmed fraud cases.
