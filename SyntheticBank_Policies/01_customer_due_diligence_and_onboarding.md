# Customer Due Diligence and Onboarding Policy

**Version:** 4.1
**Owner:** Head of Customer Onboarding
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum customer due diligence ("CDD") standards that Northwind Bank N.V. ("Northwind") applies before establishing a business relationship or executing an occasional transaction with a customer, in order to identify and verify the customer and any beneficial owner and to understand the purpose and intended nature of the relationship.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all retail and SME customers onboarded by Northwind, including customers onboarded remotely through digital channels and customers served under freedom-of-services passporting in other EU/EEA member states.

## 3. Policy Statements

1.1 The Compliance Officer must ensure that no business relationship is established until the customer's identity has been identified and verified using a valid government-issued identity document or a qualified electronic identification means.

1.2 Before establishing a business relationship, Customer Onboarding staff must identify the beneficial owner of any corporate or legal-entity customer and verify that identity on the basis of documents, data, or information obtained from a reliable and independent source.

1.3 The customer must complete a Beneficial Ownership Declaration Form identifying all natural persons who ultimately own or control the customer, which the Customer Risk Engine cross-checks against sanctions and PEP databases.

1.4 Customer Due Diligence must be performed on any occasional transaction, or linked series of transactions, equal to or exceeding EUR 10,000, regardless of whether the transaction is executed in a single operation or in several operations that appear to be linked.

1.5 The intensity and frequency of due diligence measures must be proportionate to the money-laundering and terrorist-financing risk score assigned by the Customer Risk Engine at onboarding and reassessed at each periodic review.

1.6 Identification and verification of the customer, any executor, and the beneficial owner must be completed before the business relationship is established or the occasional transaction is executed.

1.7 Customer Onboarding must record the purpose and intended nature of the business relationship in the Customer Risk Engine before the first product is activated.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Customer Onboarding:** owns this policy and approves onboarding exceptions.
- **Compliance Officer:** sets minimum identification and verification standards.
- **Customer Onboarding staff:** execute CDD measures and record outcomes in the Customer Risk Engine.
