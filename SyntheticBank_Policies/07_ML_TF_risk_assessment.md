# ML/TF Risk Assessment Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Owner:** Head of Financial Crime Risk
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025
**Applies to:** Northwind Bank N.V. and all branches, agents, and outsourcing providers acting on its behalf

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Northwind identifies, assesses, and rates the money-laundering and terrorist-financing ("ML/TF") risk associated with its business, customers, products, and delivery channels.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to Northwind's enterprise-wide risk assessment and to the customer-level risk scoring performed by the Customer Risk Engine.

## 3. Policy Statements

7.1 The Head of Financial Crime Risk must conduct and document an enterprise-wide money-laundering and terrorist-financing risk assessment at least once every twelve months, taking into account customer, product, channel, and geographic risk factors.

7.2 The enterprise-wide risk assessment must assign each business line an overall risk rating of low, medium, or high, which feeds into the Customer Risk Engine's scoring model.

7.3 The Management Board must review and approve the enterprise-wide risk assessment and any material change to Northwind's risk appetite for money laundering and terrorist financing.

7.4 The Customer Risk Engine must assign each customer a risk score based on customer type, geographic exposure, product usage, and delivery channel, updated at every periodic review.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Financial Crime Risk:** owns the enterprise-wide risk assessment.
- **Management Board:** reviews and approves the risk assessment and risk appetite.
